Hardware & Software
  • Android 14 / Kotlin (Jetpack Compose): Native mobile client with Material 3, clean MVVM architecture, and accessible large touch targets (48–80dp+) designed specifically for elderly cognitive ergonomics.
  • Firebase Authentication: Google Sign-In for Caregivers & Doctors; zero-password 6-digit numeric anonymous pairing for Patients to eliminate login frustration.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Real-time reactive snapshot synchronization (`Kotlin Flow`) for instant SOS distress alerts, daily medication tracking, and multi-user data sharing.
  • Google Gemini 1.5 Flash AI: Synthesizes gameplay telemetry to compute multi-domain cognitive scores (Memory, Attention, Speed) and generates dementia-safe dynamic routines.
  • Android On-Device TTS: Paced at 0.85x speed for gentle voice reminders and agitation de-escalation in 9 North East regional languages (Assamese, Bengali, Mizo, etc.).
  • Android Exact AlarmManager: Hardware-level reliable medication reminders that ring even when the app is closed or offline.
  • Jetpack DataStore: UID-scoped local storage ensuring fast offline boot (<1s) and data privacy across shared family devices.
