# SmritiSaathi - Wear OS Smartwatch Companion App

## Overview
The SmritiSaathi Wear OS companion app provides a streamlined, distraction-free wrist interface specifically for elderly dementia patients:
1. **Always-Accessible SOS**: Single-tap emergency alert button syncing directly to `/patients/{id}/behavioralAlerts` and notifying family members immediately.
2. **Medication & Routine Synchronization**: Displays the next scheduled medication with large, simple **Taken ✅** and **15m Snooze ⏰** response triggers.
3. **Optimized for Battery & Legibility**: High-contrast OLED dark theme with large touch targets complying with Wear OS Material design principles.

*Note: Per product specifications, full watch feature parity and watchOS (Apple Watch) are out of scope for this build and documented as future scope to keep all development strictly on 100% free-tier tooling.*
